Add `size` prop to `Badge` with `small` (10px text, 16px line height) and `medium` options, and a `subtle` prop that renders a lighter style for the `primary` variant. Badge colors and sizes are now defined in per-variant and per-size style maps, so additional styles (such as `subtle` for other variants) can be added per variant.
